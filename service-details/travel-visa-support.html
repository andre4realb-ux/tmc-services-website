Travel & Visa Support

Prepare your travel enquiry with clear information.

TMC welcomes enquiries about travel planning and visa-related consultancy support. Requirements vary by destination and individual circumstances. No visa approval or travel outcome is guaranteed; confirm the exact service and applicable requirements before paying any fee.

How we can help

  • Discuss destination and travel purpose
  • Ask what documentation information may be relevant
  • Enquire about travel planning support
  • Confirm service scope, fees and responsibilities in advance

Diaspora & International Passenger Support

For diaspora travelers, international visitors and other passengers, enquire about airport meet-and-greet, airport-to-hotel or accommodation transfers, assistance during a stay, and optional escort support for appointments, business visits, shopping or other movements. Availability, transport, personnel, costs and arrangements must be confirmed in advance. These services do not guarantee personal safety or prevent every risk.

Discuss your requirements

Every project and enquiry is different. Contact TMC to discuss your needs, confirm availability, and clarify the scope and any applicable fees before proceeding.
